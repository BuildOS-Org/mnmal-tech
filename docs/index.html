Questions

What clients usually ask.

How much does an app cost?

It depends on what the app needs to do. After the brief we send a written quote for an agreed scope, so you know the cost before any work starts.

Who owns the app when it's finished?

You do. Once the project is paid for, the code, the designs and the app store listings belong to you.

Can I pay in stages?

Yes. Payment is usually split across the stages of the project, so you pay as you see progress.

Will you sign a non-disclosure agreement?

Yes. We're happy to sign one before you share the details of your idea.

Can you work on an app someone else built?

Yes. We review the existing code first and tell you honestly whether it's better to fix it or rebuild it.

What happens after launch?

We offer support plans that cover fixes, updates, hosting and new features. If you'd rather take the code to another team, you can.
